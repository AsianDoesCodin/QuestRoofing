How it works

Request, inspect, estimate, build.

Four practical checkpoints keep the roof decision clear from first contact through closeout.

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell us what you are seeing, where the property is, and how urgent the concern feels.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    We document the visible roof condition with photos and note the areas that need attention.

  3. 03

    Estimate

    You receive a written scope with repair or replacement context before work is scheduled.

  4. 04

    Build

    Approved work is scheduled, completed, cleaned up, and reviewed before closeout.

Step by step

What happens in each step.

01

Request

  • Submit photos or describe the issue.
  • Share the property location and roof concern.
  • We confirm the best next step.
02

Inspect

  • Document roof surfaces, details, and visible damage.
  • Check leak paths, materials, penetrations, and transitions.
  • Explain findings in practical language.
03

Estimate

  • Receive a written scope of work.
  • Review repair, replacement, material, and timing options.
  • Approve the plan before scheduling.
04

Build

  • Approved work is scheduled with the crew.
  • Quality install and cleanup are handled during the job.
  • Final review confirms the work is closed out.
