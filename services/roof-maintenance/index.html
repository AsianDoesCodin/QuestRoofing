Maintenance FAQ.

How often should I schedule roof maintenance?

Many Arizona homeowners benefit from a yearly review, especially before or after monsoon season.

What is included in roof maintenance?

Quest reviews visible roof condition, common leak points, drainage concerns, material movement, and maintenance priorities.

Can maintenance prevent every leak?

No. It reduces risk by finding visible issues early, but severe weather and hidden conditions can still create problems.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Quest can discuss scheduled roof care options and the right cadence for your roof type and condition.
