Why metal roofing works in Arizona.

Metal roof decisions usually come down to lifespan, heat performance, detailing, and the look the homeowner wants.

Heat reflectivity

Metal roofing can support a more energy-conscious roof assembly when specified correctly.

Long service life

Durable panels can be a strong fit for homeowners planning long term.

Loose fasteners

Fastener movement or aging can create leak paths on exposed-fastener systems.

Panel movement

Expansion, contraction, or loose sections need a detail-focused review.

Valley detail concern

High-flow areas require clean metal work and careful water control.

Modern finish options

Color, profile, and trim choices shape both performance and curb appeal.
